The Alpha Hacker

Pure L-Theanine

There's a particular kind of mental static that shows up right before it matters — the running commentary, the rehearsing, the sudden awareness of your own hands. L-Theanine is studied for its role in supporting alpha-wave activity, the brain state you're in when you're relaxed but wide awake. This is the anchor of the whole formula, and it's dosed like it.

The Autonomic Stabilizer

Motherwort Extract

Your brain reads your heart rate to decide how worried it should be. Racing pulse in, "we're in danger" out. Motherwort extract binds the GABA-A receptor — the same neural brake prescription anxiolytics pull, through a far gentler mechanism — which is why herbalists reached for it four centuries before anyone could explain it.

The Dopamine Drip

Acetyl L-Tyrosine

Acute stress burns through your catecholamines fast. Drive, wit and verbal fluency run on those, which is why you can prepare for two weeks and still go blank in the room. ALT is the bioavailable precursor — it tops up the tank while you're actively emptying it.

The Word-Recall Engine

Uridine Monophosphate

You knew the word. You've used it a thousand times. It just wasn't there when you reached for it. That's a synaptic retrieval problem, not an intelligence problem — and uridine is a direct precursor to the phospholipids your neurons build membranes out of.

The Catalyst

Micro-Dosed Caffeine

Enough to light the engine. Nowhere near enough to make things worse. Caffeine on its own is a terrible idea before something that already has your heart going — paired with a proper dose of L-Theanine, the jagged edge comes off and what's left is clean, usable focus.

What exactly is Social Phix?
