The Obvious Questions

What exactly is Social Phix?

A 100ml shot, formulated by a licensed pharmacist, that fits in a jacket pocket. Four active compounds chosen to help support a calm physical state without trading away mental sharpness — which is the trade every other option on the shelf makes.

Will this make me feel tired or drowsy?

No. That's the entire point. Sedatives make you calm by making you slow, which is a terrible deal when you need to be sharp in twenty minutes. L-Theanine paired with a controlled micro-dose of caffeine is designed to support alpha-wave activity — the settled-but-awake state — rather than putting you under.
