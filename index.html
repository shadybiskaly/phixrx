A few things to know.

What is Social Phix?

Social Phix is a functional shot in development for the moments before presentations, interviews, networking and first dates. The goal is calm and focus. The launch pack is six shots for US$42 with free US shipping.

What ingredients are you using?

We are building around L-theanine, Sceletium extract, saffron extract and caffeine. Final extracts and amounts will be published when the formula is ready. Read the ingredient research.

Can I buy Social Phix now?

No. This is an early-access list for a product in development. The launch pack is six shots for US$42 with free US shipping. There is no checkout, charge or subscription.

Will everyone receive a free sample?

No. We may invite some early members to apply for testing if suitable samples become available. Quantities, eligibility and timing aren't confirmed.

Have you tested it?

Shady has tried the individual ingredients himself. That experience helped shape Social Phix, but it is not a test of the finished combination. The shot still needs formulation and testing before we can give reliable guidance on its effects, timing or tolerability.

Do I have to refer friends?

No. Sharing is optional and isn't required to join. The thank-you page explains the existing referral program separately.

When will it launch?

There is no confirmed date. We'll share development updates and any future purchase opportunity by email.
