You know exactly three people here and one of them is already deep in conversation. There's a cash bar, because there's always a cash bar, and it is about to become the single worst decision available to you.

Why Two Hours Feels Like Eight

Because you're not just talking. You're running a continuous background process — where do I put my hands, is this going on too long, is he looking for someone better to talk to, what was her name, what was her name — and that process is expensive. All that monitoring is exhausting. You can leave a two-hour event feeling like you did a day's work.

And the cash bar? It offers a tempting shortcut. But you came here for the conversations, the names and the judgement about when to stop talking. Those are expensive things to gamble on another drink. You've traded sharpness for ease, and at a professional event that's a bad trade at any price.
